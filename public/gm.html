    NPC Companies

    Companies whose Run by is NPCs look after themselves every time you advance time: they hire workers for their workplaces, stock their factories from their own depots and the bulk market, then list what they produce on the town's bulk market, adjust their prices as it sells or doesn't, and ship goods with no local market to a depot in a town that trades them. Nothing is spawned: every worker, input and coin comes from somewhere. Click a company to change its behaviour or see everything about it.

    Import NPC Companies

    One row per workplace; rows with the same Company build one company. Each workplace gets its own <Workplace> Store (its inputs and outputs), and if you name a Depot the store hauls its outputs there. Goods, resource nodes and recipes must already exist (Markets and Resources tabs). Workplaces that already exist are skipped, so you can re-import a sheet after adding rows. If any row has a problem, nothing is imported.

      Workplaces

      Where people work. Set the positions each one needs. Output follows the staffing bands: 100%+ staffed → 100%, 75–99 → 75%, 50–74 → 50%, 25–49 → 25%, under 25% → cannot operate. Missing Skilled or Specialist workers cap output further. Workplaces are built through Construction (a project from a workplace blueprint); once one is finished, set it up below.

      Set up a Workplace

      Nothing to set up yet. Start a project from a workplace blueprint in the Construction tab; when its last milestone is delivered, the finished workplace appears here.

      Output goes into (and inputs come from) the linked stockpile. Base output is at 100% staffing, before quality and accessibility.

      Resource Nodes

      Deposits and renewable sources, one resource (RU good) each. Output = Base Output × Staffing × Quality (Poor 0.75 · Normal 1 · Rich 1.25) × Accessibility (Easy 1.25 · Normal 1 · Difficult 0.75). Finite nodes run down their reserve. Renewables lose yield when extracted above their sustainable limit and recover when rested. Undiscovered nodes are hidden from players.

      Shipping Rules

      Players and NPC companies ship goods from a stockpile to a depot in another town. The sender picks how many days it takes, within these limits, and pays freight in the base currency: rate × units × (1 + rush % × days faster than the slowest). The money leaves circulation. Nothing is lost on the way.

      Shipments
